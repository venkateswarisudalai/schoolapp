Delete your account and data

Mayuri Kids Villa · com.schoolapp.mayuri

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and the data held about you or your child in the Mayuri Kids Villa app. Any parent, teacher, or staff member with an account may make this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to venkateswari1095@gmail.com with the subject line Account deletion request.
  2. Include the name and login ID (for example mkp-lkg-01) of the account you want deleted, and the name of your school.
  3. We may contact your school administrator to confirm the request came from you. This protects against someone else deleting a child's records.
  4. Once confirmed, the account and its associated data are deleted within 30 days, and you receive an email confirming it is done.

You can also ask your school administrator directly. Administrators can delete parent, teacher, and student accounts from within the app, and that deletion takes effect immediately.

What the school may keep

Attendance registers and fee payment records belong to the school and may be kept as part of its own records after your account is deleted, where the school is required to retain them. These are held by the school, not used for any other purpose, and are not shared with anyone else.

If you want these removed as well, say so in your email and we will pass the request to your school administrator, who decides what the school is able to delete.

Deleting the app

Uninstalling the app from your phone does not delete your account. Your data stays until a deletion request is completed using the steps above.

Questions

For anything about your data, contact venkateswari1095@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
