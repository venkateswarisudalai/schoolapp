Privacy Policy

Mayuri Kids Villa · Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mayuri Kids Villa app ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when administrators, teachers, and parents use the App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

The App is a school operations tool used by a school to manage its own students, staff, and parents. It is not directed at the general public, and accounts are created and managed by the school's administrators.

1. Information We Collect

To provide school management features, the App collects and stores:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and role (admin, teacher, or parent) used to sign in and control access.
  • Student records — student name, admission number, date of birth, gender, class/grade, enrolment date, attendance history, and photo, entered by school staff.
  • Health information about students — blood group, allergies, medications, medical conditions, and the child's doctor's name and phone number. These fields are optional and are recorded so staff can respond appropriately in a medical situation.
  • Guardian and emergency contacts — names, relationships, and phone numbers for parents/guardians, emergency contacts, and adults authorised to collect a child.
  • Fee and payment records — fee amounts, due dates, payment status, and receipts maintained by the school for its own accounting. Payments are completed in your own UPI or payment app; the App never receives or stores card, bank, or UPI credentials.
  • Location (staff only) — when a staff member checks in or out of a shift, the App reads the device's GPS position to confirm they are on campus. The coordinates, accuracy, and distance from campus are stored with that attendance record as an audit trail. Location is read only at the moment of a check-in or check-out; the App never tracks location in the background, and it does not read the location of parents or students.
  • Photos, files, and documents — class and gallery photos, feed post images, profile pictures, and documents uploaded by staff.
  • Communication content — announcements, class updates, messages, daily reports, parent concerns, and app feedback.
  • Device identifiers — a push notification token for your device, stored so the school can deliver notifications to you.

The App does not contain advertising, and does not use analytics or crash reporting SDKs.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate the school management features, including:

  • Authenticating users and enforcing role-based access.
  • Recording attendance and managing student profiles.
  • Tracking fees, generating receipts, and sending payment reminders.
  • Delivering announcements and updates between school staff and parents.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, and Cloud Storage), which provides encryption in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by role-based security rules so that users can only see data they are authorized to access.

4. Children's Data

The App stores information about children as part of normal school administration. This data is entered and controlled by the school. Children do not create accounts or use the App directly; it is operated by school staff and parents/guardians. We collect children's information only to provide school services and never for advertising or marketing.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not transfer it to third parties for their own purposes. Information is visible only to authorised users of the school, according to their role.

We use the following service providers, which process data only on our instructions in order to run the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging) — hosting, storage, sign-in, and push notifications.
  • EmailJS — used only to email app feedback you submit to the developer. The feedback message, your name, email address, and role are included in that email.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained for as long as the school maintains an active account. School administrators can update or delete student, staff, and fee records within the App.

You can request deletion of your account and its associated personal data at school-c0203.web.app/delete-account.html, or by emailing us at the address in section 9. Records the school is required to keep for its own statutory or accounting obligations may be retained for as long as that obligation lasts.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Parents and staff may request such changes through their school administrator or by contacting us directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
